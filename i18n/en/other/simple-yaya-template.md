# SimpleYAYA Template

> Reposted from [AYAYA Wiki](https://emily.shillest.net/ayayaold/)

## SimpleYAYA Template Download Page

### What is this?

A simple YAYA template ghost.<br>
It was created with reference to [Konno Ayame](http://umeici.onjn.jp/), [Hello YAYA World (Konnoyayame)](http://ms.shillest.net/yayame.xhtml), and Bun Wiki.<br>

For information on template-specific functions, see [System Dictionary/yaya_shiori3.dic](../system/yaya-shiori3-dic.md), [System Dictionary/yaya_optional.dic](../system/yaya-optional-dic.md), and the included readme.txt.

This is for people who know how to write YAYA or beginners willing to look things up on their own.<br>
If you are looking for a more comprehensive template, [Hello YAYA World (Konnoyayame)](http://ms.shillest.net/yayame.xhtml) may be a better choice.

## Features

- Despite its name, Simple has the foundation needed for a typical ghost.
- It shows which events can be omitted.
- The shell is just a square. The talk has no personality.
- It has only three dictionary files.
- Included events and resources: "translate," "choices," "startup and shutdown," "handover," "events related to multiple instances," "vanishing," "random talk," "mouse responses (double-click and stroke)," "key responses," "minimize," "time-based events (being clipped and overlapping)," "shell change," "menu definition resource," "installation," "mail check," "network update," "headline sense," "RSS," "file creation," "clock synchronization," "URL drop," and "file drop"

## Who this is for

- You feel overwhelmed when you see a template with many files.
- The more helpful and comprehensive a template is, the harder it is to modify.
- [Hello YAYA World (Konnoyayame)](http://ms.shillest.net/yayame.xhtml) was not a good fit for you.
- You feel a little guilty about overwriting a cute shell...
- You want to create as much as possible on your own.
- You have experience creating ghosts.
- You want a template that makes it easy to see what does what.
- You want to create a ghost quickly.
- You want to make a one-off gag ghost.

## Terms of use

There are basically no restrictions on modifying this template to create and distribute a ghost.<br>
You are not required to include the template's name in readme.txt or on the distribution site, or to link to this page.<br>
However, to avoid confusion when distributing a ghost you create, be sure to replace all of the following: the ghost name in "readme.txt" and "install.txt," the directory name, and the author name, ghost name, and each character name in "descript.txt".<br>
